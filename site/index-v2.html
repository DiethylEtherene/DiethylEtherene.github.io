About

I’m a first-year MEng Design Engineering student at Imperial College London, having previously studied Maths, Further Maths, Chemistry and an EPQ at Concord College. I like that the course doesn’t make me choose between the sketchbook and the CAD file — drawing something because it looks right, then figuring out why it actually works. A year in, it’s already opened up more directions than I expected.
