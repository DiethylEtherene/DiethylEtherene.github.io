CleanShift — transformational footwear

SEP – DEC 2025SOLO
  • USER RESEARCH
  • MORPHOLOGICAL ANALYSIS
  • PROTOTYPING

A shoe that carries its own overshoe. People who move between muddy ground and clean indoor floors — arctic field researchers, post-earthquake rescue crews, delivery drivers — either change footwear constantly or track the outside in. CleanShift’s cover folds into pouches on the side of the shoe, rolls out over the sole, and pulls tight with a drawstring, so you walk in without taking anything off.

I sketched fifteen mechanisms, scored them, and took three forward. Idea 3 died on the print bed — I modelled the sliding sole in Fusion 360, printed it, and found the tolerance had to be either loose enough to rattle or tight enough to squeeze mud out the sides. The one that survived was the one I could prove: I timed the fold with a reusable shower cap standing in for the cover, and it came in under the fifteen-second target I’d set.

Hero Shot replica

2024A-LEVEL EPQ
  • CAD
  • SECTIONING FOR PRINT
  • FUNCTIONAL BUILD

My extended project: a replica of the Hero Shot from Splatoon 3 — modelled from scratch, sectioned for printing, and semi-built into a working water gun. Modelling it was the obvious half. The harder half is that game weapons are drawn by artists who never have to fit anything inside them, so I had to break the model into printable sections, put the split lines where the form would hide them rather than across a visible face, and find room for real internals inside a shell whose proportions were already fixed by the source. Getting components to physically fit inside a shape that was never designed to hold them turned out to be the actual engineering problem.

ACE workshop induction redesign

2025 – 26TEAM OF 4
  • USER RESEARCH
  • SERVICE DESIGN
  • PROTOTYPE BUILD

The induction you have to clear before using the ACE workshops wasn’t landing, so we mapped the whole thing end to end — interviews, observation, journey mapping — to find where students were actually getting stuck. What came out was a scenario-based induction with knowledge checks built into it, plus evaluation criteria and a rollout path for the next academic year. Four of us worked the research and the system design. I built the game.

Entia — home test packaging

JAN – MAR 2026TEAM PROJECT
  • PACKAGING
  • COST CONSTRAINTS
  • CONCEPT DEVELOPMENT

Entia’s founder brought a set of live problems with his home blood-testing product to a student society — packaging, the test itself, data security — and people applied to whichever strand they wanted. I took packaging. The constraint stack was the interesting part: protect a delicate device, present well enough for a medical product, ship and store efficiently, stay inside a real cost target. Those pull against each other constantly, so most of the work was deciding what to trade, and where.
