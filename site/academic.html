CleanShift — transformational footwear

SEP – DEC 2025YEAR 1 COURSEWORK
  • USER RESEARCH
  • MORPHOLOGICAL ANALYSIS
  • PROTOTYPING

A shoe that carries its own overshoe. People who move between muddy ground and clean indoor floors either change footwear constantly or track the outside in, so CleanShift’s cover folds into pouches on the side of the shoe, rolls out over the sole and pulls tight with a drawstring. Fifteen mechanisms sketched and scored, three taken forward, one proved on the bench: the fold timed under the fifteen-second target I’d set. Four A3 sheets and the process PDF are on the full entry.

Entia’s founder brought live problems with his home blood-testing product to a student society and people applied to whichever strand they wanted.
