INTRO

I’m a first-year MEng Design Engineering student at Imperial College London, having previously studied Maths, Further Maths, Chemistry and an EPQ at Concord College. I like that the course doesn’t make me choose between the sketchbook and the CAD file — drawing something because it looks right, then figuring out why it actually works. A year in, it’s already opened up more directions than I expected.

02

WHAT I DO

Most of what I work on comes back to one question: how people actually move through or use something, not just what looks efficient on paper. That’s shown up in a factory floor layout I reworked this summer, a desk organiser I designed and 3D-printed from scratch — the model spinning on the front page — and helping other students think through their own design projects.

03

OUTSIDE OF COURSEWORK

Outside of that, I draw, world-build for original characters, and mess around with 3D modelling — sometimes replicating game artifacts just to see if I can nail them. This site is also vibecoded: I directed the design and built it with Claude, which turned out to be its own kind of design process.
